FAQ

Is it free to use?
Yes. ClipGrab works in your browser for public media from supported social platforms and other public websites. No account is required.
Why did a download fail?
Usually one of: the video is private, it's restricted in your country, it's been deleted, or the source platform is briefly blocking requests. Try again in a few minutes.
Do you keep a copy of my videos?
No. Nothing is stored on our servers — files are pulled from the original platform each time.
Is there an Android app?
The Android app is coming soon. For now, ClipGrab works in your browser on desktop and mobile.
